FOXBOX VOICE (BETA): first open

1. Drag FoxBox Voice into Applications (the folder beside it), then open it from Applications.

2. FoxBox Voice isn't from the App Store, so macOS stops it the first time:
   - macOS 15 Sequoia or later: open FoxBox Voice once and click Done, then go to
     System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway" next to FoxBox Voice.
   - macOS 14 Sonoma: right-click FoxBox Voice > Open > Open.
   You only do this once.

3. Enter your beta invite code (FBV-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX). Each code is for one Mac.

4. The first launch downloads the voice model (about 0.4 GB) and needs about 2 GB free.

Beta. Mac only: needs Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
FoxBox Voice installs beside FoxBox and keeps its own settings, exports and updates.
