# Type a line. Get a finished vocal.
- Type or record: a typed line in a built-in voice, or your own take with a 3-beat count-in that stops at the bar count.
- Shape it: seven presets and four macros (DEPTH, GRIT, MACHINE, SPACE) on a rack of voice effects.
- Cut to the bar: 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars, or AUTO, at your BPM and key, with the last word on the beat.
- Ready for your DAW: AIFF or WAV with BPM and key tags, plus a rekordbox.xml. Drag the finished vocal straight out.
- Beta: Mac (Apple silicon) only, unlocked with your invite code. It runs beside FoxBox with its own settings, exports and updates.
